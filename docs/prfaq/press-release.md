# Jet Skirmish press release

**Jet Skirmish brings jetpack arena battles to any browser, with no account and no install**

*Canberra, November 2026.* Jet Skirmish launches today as a free browser arena
shooter for a handful of friends. Players run through tunnels, fly over cover
on a limited jetpack, switch between three weapon slots, and carry their own
team's flag into the opposing goal. One person opens a room and shares a
five-character code, and up to eight people can be fighting in the same arena
within a minute.

**The problem.** Friends who grew up on Mini Militia still want that kind of
quick, chaotic match, but getting everyone into one game usually means app
installs, accounts and a long explanation. Jet Skirmish takes a link instead.

**The game.** It keeps the feel people remember: directional flight, fuel to
manage, a shotgun that wants you close and a sniper that wants you far away.
Maps, characters, effects and branding are original, and every weapon is
available from the first match. There are no unlocks, no accounts and no
payments.

**Flag Delivery** is the central mode. Each team carries *its own* flag into
the enemy's goal. A dropped flag stays where it fell until a teammate recovers
it, so escorts, interceptions and recoveries matter for the whole round. A
server referees every shot and delivery, so every screen agrees on who scored.

> *Draft quote for Adithya to rewrite in his own words:* "I wanted the game I
> played with friends, in a link I could send them. Joining shouldn't need an
> account or an explanation from me."

**What's in it.** Five modes (Free-for-all, Team deathmatch, Flag Delivery,
Survival and Training), thirteen maps including the zero-gravity Lunarcy, 21
firearms plus a machete and riot shield, five throwables, bots at three
difficulties, and a pilot workshop for building your own character. It plays
with keyboard and mouse, keyboard only, or touch, and remembers your pilot,
controls and match history in the same browser.

**Getting started.** Open the link, rename your pilot if you like, and create
or join a room. New players can start in Training, which teaches movement,
equipment and flag delivery against bots.

Jet Skirmish is a development build. Balance and enjoyment still need human
playtesting.
