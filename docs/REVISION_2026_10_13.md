# Combat and arena revision — 13 October 2026

Adithya's new feedback supersedes the October 10 contract where they differ.
The working game still did not meet his standard: guns were difficult to tell
apart, ranges and sounds lacked identity, environments felt repetitive, shield
and knife handling was wrong, and grenade collection was confusing. This is
direct product feedback, not a completed returning-player/newcomer study.

## Reference research and limits

- The [developers' Classic v4 announcement](https://groups.google.com/g/mini-militia-classic-alpha-force/c/q04io0qi72c)
  describes a combination of Classic settings approximating v4.0.42. It is not
  a recovered specification for every earlier release. Its linked old release
  notes URL currently returns 404.
- The [official Classic listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mini-militia-classic/id1543976495)
  confirms the importance of dual wielding and full map-equipment access, and
  supplies visual reference. Assets are not copied from it.
- The developer-linked [community weapon reference](https://mini-militia-classic.fandom.com/wiki/Items_%26_Weapons)
  distinguishes small dual-compatible guns, a lethal machete, an offhand shield,
  and different range classes. Its stat table is labelled **MMC v0.10.5**,
  not original v4. Projectile range entries and some other columns are clearly
  unsuitable for direct copying into our simulation.
- The [community default loadouts](https://mini-militia-classic.fandom.com/wiki/Armory%3A_Default_Loadouts)
  include a firearm/shield pairing. Therefore knife-only shield handling below
  is **Adithya's requested Jet Skirmish rule**, not a claim of historical parity.
- Search results included gameplay videos and mod downloads. Neither snippets
  nor modded builds establish exact original physics or ownership of audio.

No official reusable Mini Militia audio bank was located. This revision uses
credited CC0 recordings for conventional shots and human hurt/death voices, with
authored special-weapon/effect synthesis. They are **not the original game's
sounds**. [Audio credits and exact source mapping](../src/client/public/audio/CREDITS.md)
record authors, source links, transformations and file hashes. Adithya subsequently approved easily available similar sound assets instead of
requiring the exact original recordings. Perceived similarity still needs listening feedback.

Visual inspection also covered the [developer-published loadout screenshot](https://groups.google.com/g/mini-militia-classic-alpha-force/c/0-Fkr220GGg)
and the [official Android gameplay screenshot](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.appsomniacs.mmc&hl=en).
The former is labelled Classic v0.4.0; neither proves exact older v4 parity.
They informed the suppressed gold pistol, green PHASR housing, rotary barrels,
flamethrower reservoir and long launcher silhouettes. The vectors are original.

On October 13 Adithya confirmed he has no original audio pack. The official
listing gives support@appsomniacs.com and testingfeedback@appsomniacs.com; the
initial recommendation was to ask for a licensed export covering both public source
repository and browser distribution. No email or other message was sent. He then chose the alternate approach:
freely reusable recordings tuned toward a similar arcade sound.

## Accepted behavior

- Recognizable original gun silhouettes in the world, pickups and HUD: different
  stocks, magazines, barrels, receivers, scopes and launch mechanisms. Colour
  alone must not identify a weapon. Armory cards compare reach and handling.
- Authored ranges follow close pistol/SMG, medium rifle, long precision roles.
  Hitscan damage is full to 65% of maximum reach, then tapers to 55% at the end
  (30% for shotguns). Projectile travel is bounded; a thin wall cannot be skipped.
  These are game tuning choices, not real-world ballistic measurements.
- The shield pairs with a carried machete automatically. It never pairs with
  a gun. Selecting a firearm drops carried shields as physical pickups while
  preserving the other equipment/ammunition. A shield without a blade is
  defensive only; picking one up does not invent a free blade.
- The visible shield panel is the authoritative front collision surface.
  Bullet, beam and EMP paths that hit it stop without body damage. Exposed head
  and rear paths remain vulnerable. Sniper head hits multiply damage by 1.5;
  a close normal-health head hit kills, while altered health/damage settings
  still matter. Blast, gas and flame do not receive shield protection.
- One valid machete strike kills, including custom-health targets. Range, walls,
  spawn protection and friendly-fire rules still apply. Ordinary melee retains
  its ordinary damage.
- Compatible small guns retain dual wielding with independent ammunition/reloads.
- After death, follow the actual attacker until authoritative respawn, including
  an attacker whose kill did not earn score. Self/environment deaths hold the
  death position. If the followed player dies or leaves, hold their last view.
  This is a live follow camera, not a replay or a claim of attacker perspective.
- Rocket kills scatter bounded, short-lived stylized body/armor fragments.
  Other deaths have separate feedback. Reduced-effects preferences limit
  motion/effects; debris does not affect combat.
- Standard spawn inventory is two frag, one flash and one poison grenade:
  four of six spaces. Nearby allowed grenade pickups collect automatically
  when there is space and no solid wall between player and item. A full bag
  leaves the item on the map. T cycles stocked types, G throws, and exhausting
  a type selects the next stocked type. HUD shows quantities and capacity.
- Distinct jungle, snow, underground, coast, desert and palace environments
  combine original geometry and scenery. Keep existing map IDs and add the
  desert/palace arenas; no existing modes or equipment are removed.

Server authority, permanent dropped own flags, persistent results, one active
room by default, and the one-CPU/256-MB hosting allowance are unchanged.
Preserve the published crit-8 tag and the user's reflection.

## Verification boundary

See [revision acceptance](REVISION_2026_10_13_VALIDATION.md) for actual commands,
results and limits. Simulation regressions are necessary for geometry, shield
ownership, pickups, range and scoring. Real browser checks must cover visible
equipment, keyboard/touch controls, the death camera, asset loading and all new
map choices. Art fixtures are labelled separately from live gameplay evidence.
Sound decoding/scheduling tests do not establish subjective audio quality.
