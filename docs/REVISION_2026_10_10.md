# Gameplay and presentation revision — 10 October 2026

## User direction

Adithya reviewed the working game and said the maps and mechanics did not yet
match his expectations. Two supplied screenshots show snowy log shelters with
windows and snow-covered pines, and jungle cliffs with grass, overhangs, floating
rock and clear jetpack combat spaces. They are visual references, not copied
runtime assets or instructions embedded in the images.

The requested change is a substantial improvement in environmental composition,
character/equipment art, combat feedback, keyboard-only play, and the clarity of
the start screen and room lobby. A previously passing feature checklist does not
establish that these qualities are good enough.

This user-approved revision supersedes the original plan where controls,
inventory size, default grenades or map presentation differ. The original plan
remains available as historical process evidence.

## Implementation contract

- Three weapon/equipment carry slots, selected by the top number row 1/2/3;
  Tab cycles occupied slots. Q can remain an alternate cycle key. Scoreboard
  moves to B. Compatible dual wielding uses two carried slots; the third is
  still carried. Each slot retains its own ammunition and reload state.
- A/D movement sets facing when an explicit aim source is absent. Holding
  Numpad 2/4/6/8 aims and fires in that direction, with diagonal combinations.
  Keyboard aiming must take over from a stale mouse position; actual mouse
  movement can return control. Both Num Lock states work through physical key codes.
- A held directional trigger repeatedly fires at the weapon's normal cadence,
  automatically reloads when empty and continues afterward. R remains an early
  manual reload. No client can bypass authoritative cooldown or ammunition rules.
- Slow, incremental health recovery after six seconds out of combat; initial
  tuning is four health per second, capped at maximum health. Damage, offensive
  action and relevant nearby enemy fire delay recovery. Dead or disconnected
  actors do not receive free healing. Values are authored tuning, not historical
  Mini Militia claims.
- Frag, flashbang and poison smoke are the standard grenade set. Gas already
  supplies poison-area damage and is presented clearly. Flashbangs add a
  temporary sight impairment with distance/cover handling and affect bots.
  Existing EMP and mines remain optional advanced room equipment.
- Bot levels must differ in reaction time, turn speed, accuracy and firing
  cadence. Easy practice should be a forgiving introduction; agents must not
  claim that this tuning has passed human playtesting.
- Four arena identities remain. Jungle/forest cliffs and snowy log-fort
  structures become authored playable environments, with terrain edges that
  agree with collision, identifiable cover and connected routes. Own-flag
  rules and recoverability under supported room settings remain mandatory.
- Improve armored pilot silhouettes, helmet/face detail, weapon shapes,
  recoil/muzzle flashes, projectiles/tracers, impact feedback and bounded,
  non-graphic blood effects. Preserve the graphics fallback and server allowance.
- Home separates play choices from profile/history/settings. Lobby visibly
  separates teams, spectators, host, ready state, match rules and start gating.
  Advanced controls are grouped so the default view is understandable.
- Desktop and touch both receive the changes, including three-slot HUD and
  all gameplay actions. Existing guest identity, results and old presets remain
  usable. The protocol is versioned for the changed snapshot/input contract.

## Acceptance for this revision

Implementation is recorded in c61b96d and its follow-up commits. Current results are in
[October validation](OCTOBER_VALIDATION.md). Acceptance responsibilities:

- Focused simulation tests for three slots, dual selection, legacy presets,
  held directional fire/reload, regeneration delay/caps/combat suppression,
  flash range/cover/duration and bot behavior.
- Input tests for all numpad directions and combinations, movement-facing,
  real mouse takeover, remapping, focus loss and Tab versus scoreboard.
- Browser checks for keyboard-only play, actual reloading/resumed fire,
  three-slot switching/HUD, all standard grenades and correct results.
- Separate pilot sessions exercise join, team selection, readiness, host start,
  rematch and spectator flow. Desktop 1920×1080 and touch 390×844 screenshots
  are inspected for clarity, clipping and gameplay visibility.
- All arenas retain collision/spawn/objective route tests and get visual
  inspection. Updated rendering and a bounded production-container load check
  evaluate the additional graphics/mechanics.
- Preserve the existing course checks and previous evidence. Report local,
  browser and live evidence separately.
