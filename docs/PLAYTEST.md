# Human playtest record

Status: awaiting human playtests. Automated checks do not establish familiarity,
fairness, or fun. Do not fill observations from an agent's predictions.

Use one returning Mini Militia player and one newcomer. Record the build commit,
date, browser/device, approximate connection quality, and whether this was local
or deployed. Do not record unnecessary personal information.

Observe each participant joining an invited room, identifying their team,
moving/flying/depleting and recovering fuel, picking up/switching/reloading guns,
selecting and throwing grenades, explaining whose flag they carry, completing a
delivery, recovering a dropped flag, reading MVP/results, rematching, and
returning to their saved history.

Record observed confusion, mistakes, successful recoveries, and the participant's
own comments. Ask what felt familiar, what felt unfair, and whether they wanted
another match. A pass/fail checklist alone is insufficient for these judgements.
